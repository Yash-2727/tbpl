THE BLUE PAPER LAB — Aquarium 7-in-1
Version 1.4.2 — Strip Orientation Verification

Changes:
- Added a manufacturer-profile-aware orientation gate before colour analysis.
- Compares the detected seven-pad sequence in normal and reversed order.
- Blocks analysis when the reversed sequence clearly matches better (likely 180-degree upside-down upload).
- Blocks analysis when orientation cannot be distinguished confidently.
- Displays forward/reversed match scores and an actionable retake instruction.
- Re-checks orientation immediately before analysis.
- Saves orientation assessment with each replicate record.
- Preserves the v1.4.1 UI, CIEDE2000 matching, robust sampling, duplicate-photo checks, and report workflow.

Expected Aquarium 7-in-1 order (top to bottom):
1. Nitrate (NO3)
2. Nitrite (NO2)
3. Chlorine (Cl2)
4. Total Hardness (GH)
5. Total Alkalinity (TA)
6. Carbonate Hardness (KH)
7. pH

Important limitations:
- Orientation decision is a conservative chart-colour consistency heuristic, not a trained visual classifier.
- Current chart HEX colours are visual approximations, so ambiguous images are blocked rather than guessed.
- The provisional score separation thresholds require validation with a labelled dataset of correctly oriented and upside-down strip photographs.
- Technician visual review remains mandatory.
