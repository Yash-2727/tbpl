Version 1.4.1 reliability update (November 2026)
- Preserved the existing interface, navigation, three-replicate workflow, chart profiles and CIEDE2000 matcher.
- Replaced one central sampling region with five small pad regions; each region gets a median RGB and the final pad colour is the median of region medians.
- Added channel-spread and region-count diagnostics.
- Added an 8x8 average-hash image fingerprint to flag exact/near-duplicate replicate uploads (Hamming distance <= 3). Duplicate replicate photos are blocked until replaced. This is a screening heuristic only.
- Added a provisional ambiguity flag when the best and second-best ΔE00 scores differ by less than 1.0. This is not a validated accuracy threshold and must be calibrated with instrument-verified samples.
- Added match assessment and replicate fingerprint details to downloadable reports.
- Added explicit chart-calibration guidance. Existing HEX swatches remain approximate visual estimates from photographs; the application does not claim instrument-calibrated chart colours.
- No chemical concentration accuracy claim is made. Validate against suitable reference methods/instrument readings before operational use.
